# Accounting scope and comparability rules

These rules govern whether facts may be compared or aggregated. They do not reinterpret source values.

1. Cash, modified cash, modified accrual, and full accrual remain separately identified. Aggregates do not combine different bases.
2. Government-wide, governmental-fund, proprietary-fund, fiduciary-fund, and supplementary-schedule facts retain separate reporting scopes.
3. Audited actual, unaudited actual, estimated actual, and budget amounts remain separate value types.
4. Net position, fund balance, cash, and investments are distinct measures.
5. Revenue, receipts, expenses, and disbursements are distinct measures unless an explicit, basis-specific definition establishes comparability.
6. Transfers and other intra-entity activity are excluded from external operating revenue and expenditure aggregates.
7. Debt proceeds are financing sources. Principal payments, interest, and operating costs remain distinct.
8. Depreciation is an accrual expense and is not presented as a cash disbursement.
9. Summary, combining, and detail schedules may describe the same activity. Every fact must have an explicit publication role so only one representation contributes to a given aggregate.
10. Missing information remains missing. A published zero requires direct source support for zero.

## Geographic aggregation

Entity location, primary county, service-area counties, and statewide status are separate attributes. Countywide totals may include only entities whose inclusion rule is explicit; multi-county entities are not silently assigned wholly to one county.

## Fiscal periods

The reporting period is derived from the statement and supporting source evidence, not solely from filenames or filing directories. Comparative prior-year columns and budget columns are stored separately from current-year actual values.
