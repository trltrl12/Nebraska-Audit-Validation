# Phase 4 Review Candidate Triage

The initial quality scan reported 177 active transfer-label review candidates. Of these, 176 are explicit contra, elimination, reclassification, or net-of-transfer detail facts. They remove interfund transfers from expenditure or receipt lines where the source schedule embeds those transfers, preventing the same activity from being counted both as operating activity and other financing.

These adjustment records remain in their affected operating section with a signed amount and explanatory label. They are no longer classification candidates solely because the label contains the word “transfer.” Hall County's $5,356,069 “Other Receipts Interfund Transfers” item does not identify an elimination or reclassification and remains under review.

All 22 semantic-duplicate candidates were then checked against eleven retained activity-fund pages. In every case, the source prints the same amount in separate receipt and disbursement columns for the same account. These are distinct facts whose equal values are coincidental, not duplicate records. The duplicate detector now treats a single revenue/expenditure pair as separate column facts while continuing to flag repeated records within the same section.
